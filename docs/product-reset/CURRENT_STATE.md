## Product status

The canonical implementation is the in-repository `v2/`. The approved product direction is now
**group-native**: help a recurring group of 2–6 people decide where to go using the taste history
they already have. A pair remains supported as the smallest group, not the entire product center.
Root v1 and the external clean-room prototype are historical inputs, not alternative implementations.

The recovered product lineage, competitor boundary, research implications, visual system, browser findings, and Figma handoff are consolidated in [`DESIGN_DIRECTION.md`](DESIGN_DIRECTION.md).
